# Lil' Doc Play Medic Kit (Ages 3+)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Accessory (kit — four components) |
| **Quality** | Epic |
| **Item Level** | 10 |
| **Soulbound** | Yes — [Wade](../../characters/party/wade.md) |
| **Source** | [Spirit Dungeon completion reward](../../lore/dungeons/spirit-dungeon/boss.md#completion-rewards--gifts-with-purchase) |

A plastic toy doctor kit from the costume aisle — and every piece of toy-medical junk in it *works*. A playing-doctor-with-your-kids toy, in the hands of the man who will become the Healer of San Francisco.

## Components

### Plastic Stethoscope — True Diagnosis
Press it to anyone and Wade reads their HP, status effects, diseases, and curses — and the diagnosis **cannot be refused, resisted, or deceived**. It works on strangers, the unconscious, the hostile, and reads *true* state underneath falsified state: through disguises, possession, and **mental domination**. Wade's spells show him numbers; the stethoscope shows him *why*. Party frames already cover the willing — this is triage for the unwilling, and it requires deliberately placing the chest-piece ("deep breath in"), so it is an out-of-combat-shaped act even when the patient is fighting him.

### Boo-Boo Strips — Structural Restoration
Cartoon band-aids that don't touch HP at all. They fix what HP healing *can't*: severed limbs, ruined joints, old scars, permanent injuries — Greater-Restoration-tier work, far above Wade's level. Three balance levers:
- **Slow:** hours to days, never combat-viable.
- **Reattaches, never regrows:** you need the arm.
- **Rationed:** small strips restock daily; the **one big bandage** at the bottom of the box only reappears **weekly**.

The repair permanently takes the print. Somewhere in SF is a veteran whose reattached sword arm has a faint dinosaur pattern in the skin, forever.

### Candy Pill Bottle — Portable Healing
Dispenses **5 candies per day, total** — not per person. Each is a small heal or cleanse (Level-10 cap). No channeling, no mid-fight sharing — he hands them out like an actual parent rationing sweets. *"You already had two."*

### "I WAS BRAVE" Stickers — Aura To-Go
A sticker carries **Wade's own [Cleric Aura](../../classes/cleric-of-healing.md#cleric-aura) payload** (minor fear resistance + 5% healing received) *away from him* — his calm, detached from his radius, working wherever the sticker is worn until it peels (about a day). A few regenerate on the sheet daily. The aura only works near Wade; the stickers are for everyone he can't stand next to.

## Design Notes

> **Rate-capped, not scope-capped.** The kit's [Dungeon-Tier Cap](../../lore/dungeons/spirit-dungeon/boss.md#completion-rewards--gifts-with-purchase) applies to *speed and quantity* (slow strips, 5 pills, sticker trickle), while the *scope* (structural repair, consent-proof diagnosis) exceeds Wade's level. Miracle-tier work at kid-bedtime pace.
>
> **The stethoscope is the Chekhov piece.** In Book 1 Wade gets it and shrugs — intra-party he already knows everyone's state. It exists to be *capable* of the scene where someone he loves is fighting him and he needs to see what's been done to their mind ([Eron](../../characters/villains/eron-vosk.md) is a Mind Mage; dominated victims already exist in canon). No specific future beat is committed here.
>
> **The stickers and the aura wish.** The stickers materially rhyme with Wade's Ch 22 wish ("always on, covering everyone, nobody having to ask" — [cleric-of-healing.md](../../classes/cleric-of-healing.md#cleric-aura)), which canon marks as *planted, do not return to it*. The stickers honor the plant **silently** — an item quietly inching him toward the thing he wants to be — and must never be connected to the wish on-page.
>
> **The arc beat:** Wade is the one who froze at the mirror — Broken, no shard. The man who wasn't brave hands out bravery. The party starts refusing heals unless a sticker comes with it — and somewhere down the line, someone quietly puts an "I WAS BRAVE" sticker on *Wade*.
