# Lil' Sparkler Glitter Gel Pen (Non-Toxic)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Enchanting Tool (scribe) |
| **Quality** | Epic |
| **Item Level** | 10 |
| **Soulbound** | Yes — [Clint](../../characters/party/clint.md) |
| **Source** | [Spirit Dungeon completion reward](../../lore/dungeons/spirit-dungeon/boss.md#completion-rewards--gifts-with-purchase) |

A chunky kids' glitter gel pen from the seasonal craft aisle, still on its cardboard backing. The packaging copy — **"Writes on ANYTHING!"** — turns out to be literally true.

## Properties

### Writes on Anything
Any surface accepts and holds the pen's ink: paper, steel, glass, stone, dragonhide. The ink is permanent — only the enchanter who wrote it can erase their own work. The pen never runs dry.

### Enchanting Scribe (Level 10 cap)
The pen can inscribe working enchantments **up to Level 10** ([Dungeon-Tier Cap](../../lore/dungeons/spirit-dungeon/boss.md#completion-rewards--gifts-with-purchase)). Attempting anything higher produces plain glitter — pretty, inert, no magic.

### Layering (the power is Clint, not the pen)
The pen grants no layering ability of its own. But [Clint's](../../characters/party/clint.md) hacked **Grandmaster** [Enchanting](../../professions/enchanting.md) rank lets him **stack many enchantments on a single item**, where a normal Master gets one or two. Quantity-over-tier is his path to closing the gear gap with [Eron](../../characters/villains/eron-vosk.md) at Level 25: no single rune matches a L25 enchanter's work, but a breastplate carrying nine of them does.

### The Gag (it compounds)
Every rune comes out in **neon glitter ink**, and settled runes slowly drift into **crayon-style kid drawings** — a sun wearing sunglasses, a stick-figure Clint giving a thumbs-up. The more layers on an item, the worse it gets. The party's endgame gearing arc is also the arc of the deadliest party in San Francisco slowly coming to look like a kindergarten art project. Enemies underestimate them right up until the sword with the doodle-dog on it cuts through their armor.

## Design Notes

> Canon already establishes the gap this closes: *"He has the skill, if not the materials"* ([enchanting.md](../../professions/enchanting.md)). The pen solves the **tool** half; [Celeste's](../../characters/supporting/celeste.md) forge solves the **material** half — she smiths clean, gallery-quality steel, then watches Clint cover it in glitter. Together they are the party's gearing engine for the Level-20 end-of-book boss.
>
> Bonus friction: Celeste's husband is a working Enchanter of ordinary rank. Clint showing up with Grandmaster rank and a child's gel pen makes for an awkward professional dynamic at Clayandsteel.
