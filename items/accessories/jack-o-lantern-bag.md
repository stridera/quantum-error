# Jack-o'-Lantern Bag

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Accessory (belt-attachable, drawstring) |
| **Quality** | Uncommon |
| **Item Level** | 10 (Spirit Dungeon, Room 4a) |

A cloth trick-or-treat bag with a cartoon jack-o'-lantern print and a drawstring that pulls it closed and ties it to a belt. Looks like a child's Halloween candy bag. Is actually a small pocket dimension.

## Ownership & Status

Held by [Selene](../../characters/party/selene.md) since Ch 24 · status: intact.

## Origin

Found by Selene while rummaging through the milk crates in the [janitor's closet](../../lore/dungeons/spirit-dungeon/rooms.md#room-4a-janitors-closet-optional--hidden) (Room 4a), the hidden side room she picks her way into with a hairpin during the Room 4 traverse. The room's whole purpose is to pay off Rogue play with a rest, a treasure, and a monster the party gets to walk away from; this is the treasure.

> **Naming:** earlier drafts of `rooms.md` called it the *Treat Bag of Holding*. The on-page name is **Jack-o'-Lantern Bag** and that is canonical.

## Stats

- **Effect:** A small bag of holding — roughly a large backpack's worth of storage.
- Leaves the wearer **unencumbered**.
- Not in the class of [Clint's Fanny Pack of Holding](fanny-pack-of-holding.md), but genuinely useful.

**Flavor:** *"The perfect container for your local kid who wants to hit up all the houses without ever returning home."*

> **Prose Source:** Chapter 24 — Selene turns it up in the milk crates while the party rests in the janitor's closet.
