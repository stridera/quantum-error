# Agent's Clip-On Bow Tie

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Accessory (neck) |
| **Quality** | Epic |
| **Item Level** | 10 |
| **Soulbound** | Yes — [Selene](../../characters/party/selene.md) |
| **Requires Attunement** | Yes — 3 consecutive days worn |
| **Source** | [Spirit Dungeon completion reward](../../lore/dungeons/spirit-dungeon/boss.md#completion-rewards--gifts-with-purchase) |

A cheap plastic clip-on bow tie from the spy costume set. One size fits all.

## Properties

### Doubled Stealth
While attuned, the wearer's effective Stealth is **doubled**, capped at a Level-10-equivalent bonus ([Dungeon-Tier Cap](../../lore/dungeons/spirit-dungeon/boss.md#completion-rewards--gifts-with-purchase)) — at high skill the doubling plateaus rather than scaling forever.

### The Score (the catch)
While the wearer is sneaking, a **full spy-thriller soundtrack plays**, audible to everyone within ~15 meters. Critically, the music is **non-directional** — it comes from everywhere and nowhere, and cannot be used to locate her. Enemies who hear it go **Alert** (searching, weapons out, aggro radius up) but cannot pinpoint the source. It is simultaneously the best and the most infuriating stealth item in the game.

Comedy physics: the score **swells** when she reaches for a lockpick, stings on a pickpocket, and cuts to a **record scratch** if she's spotted.

### Attunement Warm-Up
The tie only functions after **3 consecutive days worn**. Removing it — including stashing it in inventory — **resets the clock to zero**. Net effect: Selene wears a costume clip-on bow tie over Veilstepper leathers at all times, forever. She *can* take it off to sneak up on [Rebekah](../../characters/party/rebekah.md) in silence, but that trades away doubled stealth for the next three days — a decision she will agonize over on-page.

## Tactics

- **The lure:** a whole room converges on music with no source while the party flanks. The drawback is also a tool, which is very Selene.
- **The party always knows where she is.** The surprise-Selene gag is dead within the party, and she resents this.

## Design Notes

> The non-directional clause is the balance hinge: the music leaks *that* someone is sneaking, never *where*. Alert-but-not-detected keeps the doubled stealth honest without making it useless.
