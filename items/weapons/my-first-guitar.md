# My First Guitar (strings sold separately)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Instrument (bard implement) |
| **Quality** | Epic |
| **Item Level** | 10 |
| **Soulbound** | Yes — [Rebekah](../../characters/party/rebekah.md) |
| **Source** | [Spirit Dungeon completion reward](../../lore/dungeons/spirit-dungeon/boss.md#completion-rewards--gifts-with-purchase) |

A pink plastic kids' costume guitar, stickers included, **no strings** — and that's the joke and the mechanic in one: *strings sold separately, and time is the string.* Rebekah's temporal mana bridges the gap as five shimmering strands, each vibrating at a slightly different tempo. Her first real instrument.

## Properties

### Temporal Strings
The five mana-strands only exist while a [Temporal Bard](../../classes/temporal-bard.md) holds the guitar. Notes played on them hang slightly outside of time — chords sustain longer than physics allows.

### Amplifier
**+25% potency to all songs and Refrains**, capped at Level-10-equivalent output ([Dungeon-Tier Cap](../../lore/dungeons/spirit-dungeon/boss.md#completion-rewards--gifts-with-purchase)).

### The Malfunction
The cheap toy soul occasionally remembers what it was built for and **randomly slips into kid songs mid-performance** — the battle anthem lurches into *"The Wheels on the Bus."* Because her magic runs *through* the strings, **the buff keeps working**: the party gets its full combat buff, they just have to receive it via *"Old MacDonald"* while fighting for their lives. No pattern, no trigger, no off switch — once a verse starts, the only way out is through.

There is also a demo button. It cannot be disabled, because the tinny built-in speaker is load-bearing to the enchantment.

## Design Notes

> Deliberately **no layering mechanic** — Rebekah's own kit already covers layering songs; the guitar amplifies, it doesn't duplicate her class. The instrument is a potency upgrade plus a malfunction gag, nothing more.
