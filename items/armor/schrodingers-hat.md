# Schrödinger's Hat

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Armor (head) |
| **Quality** | Epic |
| **Item Level** | 10 |
| **Soulbound** | Yes — [Vanessa](../../characters/party/vanessa.md) |
| **Source** | [Spirit Dungeon completion reward](../../lore/dungeons/spirit-dungeon/boss.md#completion-rewards--gifts-with-purchase) |

A cheap costume witch hat, floppy brim, faint smell of the plastic bag it came in. The most on-theme quantum item the store could have produced.

## Properties

### Superposition Slot
Vanessa can cast a spell **into** the hat, where it exists as both cast and not-cast until she **collapses** it — releasing the stored spell **instantly, with no cast time**. One spell at a time; casting into an occupied hat fails ("the hat is full"). A stored fireball she can drop mid-melee is a major tactical unlock, and pairs directly with her [Metamagic](../../magic/schools/metamagic.md) experiments.

### Storage Cap
The slot holds spells up to a **Level-10-equivalent mana cost** ([Dungeon-Tier Cap](../../lore/dungeons/spirit-dungeon/boss.md#completion-rewards--gifts-with-purchase)). Overdrafting — say, pumping an empowered 10k-mana fireball into it — is **rejected**: the maybe-cat yowls, the hat burps a shower of harmless sparks, and the entire spell is wasted, mana spent, nothing stored. She learns this the hard way exactly once, in front of everyone.

### The Maybe-Cat
Sometimes the hat **meows**. There is a cat in there. There is *not* a cat in there. Both are true; no one has ever found the cat, and [Qubit](../accessories/mimic-pet.md) is **obsessed** — mimic-versus-maybe-cat is a recurring bit.

## Design Notes

> Deliberately does **not** replicate the ally-exclusion [Sharpshooter's Monocle](../../lore/dungeons/spirit-dungeon/mobs.md#the-100-ticket-set--the-temptation) she surrendered at the prize booth — she's grinding that effect back honestly through Metamagic (Novice 3, Ch 23), and handing it to her would cheapen both the sacrifice and the grind.
