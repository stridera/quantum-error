# Robe of the Silent Scream

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Robe (light armor) |
| **Quality** | Magic (dungeon loot) |
| **Item Level** | ~L10 (Spirit Dungeon mannequin drop) |

A lightweight black robe with long, loose sleeves and a stark white lining that flashes
when the wearer moves too quickly. It smells faintly of plastic and adrenaline.

## Ownership & Status

Held by Clint since Ch 16 (handed over by Vanessa "until we find something else") →
**damaged Ch 17** (dropbear jaws sliced the fabric across the chest) → offered to Selene
Ch 19 (the long-flagged transfer) — **declined** because of the damage → stored in the
[Fanny Pack of Holding](../accessories/fanny-pack-of-holding.md) pending repair · status:
damaged, stored; intended for Selene once repaired.

## Origin

Looted from the scream-mask/pinhead-mask mannequin pair in the Spirit Dungeon department
store (Ch 16). Vanessa pulled it from the drops and handed it to Clint, replacing his towel.

## Stats

- **+2 Evasion**
- **+2 Damage** when attacking from stealth
- **Passive:** On a critical hit, small chance to inflict **Disrupt** or **Brief Fear** on the target
