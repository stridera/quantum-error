# Singed Fedora

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Head (hat) |
| **Quality** | Common (dungeon loot) |
| **Item Level** | ~L10 (Spirit Dungeon mannequin drop) |

A brown fedora, singed along the brim, with red and green stripes hidden just inside the
lining. Once the dream of most young men, it has become a quiet nightmare for all around
them. The hat seems to hum with misplaced confidence and lingering dream-rot.

## Ownership & Status

Worn by Clint since Ch 15 ("I put on the hat") · status: intact.

## Origin

Dropped by the burned-face mannequin in the Spirit Dungeon (Ch 15).

## Stats

- Minor resistance to external mental influence
- Alters the social effects of charisma on nearby creatures
- Instills the wearer with an exaggerated sense of style and self-assurance
