# Black Robe of Silent Judgement

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Robe (reinforced cloth, caster) |
| **Quality** | Magic (dungeon loot) |
| **Item Level** | ~L10 (Spirit Dungeon mannequin drop) |

A heavy black robe stitched with subtle geometric seams. The fabric hangs perfectly still
no matter how the wearer moves, and looking at it too long makes your thoughts feel…
ordered.

## Ownership & Status

Held by Vanessa since Ch 16 ("this one is for spell casters") · status: intact.

## Origin

Looted alongside the [Robe of the Silent Scream](robe-of-the-silent-scream.md) from the
mannequin pair in the Spirit Dungeon department store (Ch 16). Vanessa claimed it as the
party's caster.

## Stats

- **+2 Damage Resistance** (Reinforced Cloth)
- **Mental Resistance (Minor)**
- **+2 Spell Damage**
- **+1 Will**
- **−2 Evasion**
- **Passive:** Mental or control effects the wearer applies last slightly longer
