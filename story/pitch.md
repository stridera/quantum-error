# Pitch — Book 1: Quantum Error

## Elevator Pitch

Five coworkers at a quantum computing startup discover reality is a simulation — and they've found the console that edits it. They boost their stats, cure a dying child, quietly become superhuman. But when a ruthless billionaire moves to seize the discovery, the lead engineer makes a desperate last play: he rewrites the world's template from "modern technology" to "high-fantasy RPG." Electricity dies. Mana floods the globe. Monsters spawn in the streets.

Now Clint the programmer is a Paladin who can't level up until he picks a god, his boss is a Cleric whose healing keeps surfacing a grief he can't explain, and their lawyer is finally living as her true self — as a catgirl rogue. The five must master their new classes and fight across a transformed Bay Area to stop the villain they accidentally turned into a goblin warlord — because a ten-year countdown to annihilation is already running.

LitRPG with a heart: stat sheets and smites on the surface; underneath, a story about who you become when the world stops pretending. Book 1 of a planned five-book series, *The Quantum Cycle*.
