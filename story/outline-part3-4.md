# Book I — Parts 3 & 4 Outline

Working outline for the back half of *Quantum Error*. Structural decisions below were confirmed by the author (2026-08-18); items under **Open drafting decisions** are deliberately unresolved and get decided on the page.

## Where things sit

- **Word budget:** ~60k at the end of Part 2 (Spirit Dungeon). Chapters run 2–2.5k. Target ~110–120k total → Part 3 ≈ 30k, Part 4 ≈ 25–30k, roughly 20–25 chapters remaining.
- **Level track:** party exits the dungeon ~L10 (completion loot is L10-capped — the dungeon's graduation level). [Eron](../characters/villains/eron-vosk.md) is a L25 Goblin Warlord designed to be fought at L20. Part 3 covers ~10 levels; every level-up is attached to a plot beat, never a grind montage.
- **Part boundary:** Part 3 ends the moment preparation is done and the party turns toward the fortress. Curtain line: *"Do you think it'll work?" / "It'll take a miracle."* — banter now; on reread, a paladin and a cleric with a silent god agreeing a miracle is required. Part 4 is the assault.
- **Broken:** clears on Spirit Dungeon exit (canon, [rooms.md](../lore/dungeons/spirit-dungeon/rooms.md) / [wade.md](../characters/party/wade.md)). Wade enters Part 3 debuff-free — but still the only party member with a cracked, unclaimed Mirror Shard. The *failure* persists; the status does not. (Shard re-attempt is reserved for a future Spirit Dungeon — not this book.)

## Part 3 engines (why it isn't connective tissue)

1. **The clock is Amanda.** Eron tracks the party through [Amanda](../characters/supporting/amanda.md)'s family-sense. Downtime in SF is downtime under surveillance: probes find them impossibly fast, ambushes know roads they told no one about. Solving the tracking mystery *is* the "learn what we're up against" homework, delivered as plot.
2. **Dan is Part 3's antagonist face.** The Spirit Dungeon's Dan-themed boss drops and Room 5 foreshadowing hand the party the thread. [Dan](../characters/villains/dan.md) harries the routes east and converts the raids' dead. Eron stays unspent for the finale; beating the lieutenant is what convinces the party they're ready for the boss.
3. **Gearing is a quest chain, not a shopping montage.** [Celeste](../characters/supporting/celeste.md) can smith and her husband can enchant, but post-Transition they need materials only an adventuring party can fetch. Every upgrade is a small expedition with its own encounter and XP. Clint's glitter-pen enchanting engine plugs in here; Clint + Celeste's husband as enchanting odd couple is a standing comedy vein.

Secondary Part 3 payload: Rebekah's "what is a silent bard for" wound (Room 4) gets its answer; Clint/Vanessa and Selene/Rebekah closeness breathes in a city with beds and dinner tables; Qubit meets civilization; Clint at Celeste's anvil while she tells him what the city's been through.

## Part 3 — San Francisco / The Road East (~30k)

- **Return to SF.** The city changed in their absence; reunions and casualty lists; establishes what home is worth defending.
- **Celeste arc opens.** Assessment of the dungeon loot; "I can build you better — bring me materials." The quest board for the part.
- **First strike.** A goblin probe finds them impossibly fast. The tracking mystery opens.
- **Material expeditions** (2–3, distinct encounter flavors). Gear lands piecewise; levels climb; tactics visibly mature versus the dungeon.
- **Forest-spirit rumor.** A mauled lower-level party is deposited at the gates by wolves, babbling about children. The parties the twins rescue are the groups that tried to push east and failed — which seeds the rumors, shows the danger gradient, and explains why nobody but the twins has intel: everyone who got close came back unconscious on a wolf. (The party is the highest-leveled group in the region; there is no one else to learn from.)
- **Undead sign.** Expeditions keep crossing Dan's work. A freed prisoner assembles the camp's rough picture: fortress, warlord, a necromancer ally — and a woman at Eron's side who isn't chained. Wade hears his wife described as *loyal*.
- **The twins' first visit.** [The twins](../characters/supporting/the-twins.md) come to their father — and won't stay. **Motive (confirmed):** the mind control looks like the disease. They spent years watching themselves forget Wade — parental recognition went first — and they are the only two people alive who know what's behind their mother's empty eyes from the inside. *"She stayed when we didn't know her. We're staying until she knows us."* Wade can't argue; it's his own value system pointed back at him. They recur through Part 3 as the party's sole intel channel — every visit ends with his daughters choosing their mother, him agreeing with the choice, and it flaying him anyway.
- **The stethoscope → the stab → Dan (Part 3 climax, one continuous sequence):**
  - An operation Amanda is guiding goes wrong for Eron's side; the party corners her. Wade won't let anyone touch her.
  - The [Plastic Stethoscope](../items/accessories/lil-doc-medic-kit.md)'s True Diagnosis requires deliberately placing the chest-piece — that designed vulnerability is the scene. Wade gets his diagnosis, and the price is her knife going in while he reads it.
  - **The diagnosis (locked mechanics):** persistent domination *lattice*, caster-keyed — **only Eron can dispel it**. Healing is useless; Wade's entire class has no lever. Crucially, the lattice has **no live tether**: Eron installed it and walked away; it reports nothing back (it's a beacon, not a leash he holds). The Part 3 diagnosis is the Part 4 playbook.
  - Amanda flees; the pursuit crashes into Dan's prepared screen of undead — the climax fight, now personal instead of lieutenant-clearing. Dan is driven off (not killed) but leaves on his own terms: Amanda lifted away on a monster mount, the party standing in a field of re-dead corpses having won the fight and lost her.
  - **Eron's arrogance, reinforced:** Dan reports what he saw. Eron now knows Wade diagnosed the lattice — which to Eron means the party has *confirmed they can't break it*. He never checks on the spell again; they told him, in effect, that checking is unnecessary.
  - Party ~L18–19 after; Dan's flight to Eron is how the fortress learns they're coming strong — raising the finale rather than deflating it.
- **The Null Zone lands after the loss.** The twins bring it: the ground the wolves refuse to walk. Despair turns into a plan.
- **Prep, compressed to a chapter.** Final Celeste/enchanter gear; the plan; L20 on the eve.
- **Curtain:** *"Do you think it'll work?" / "It'll take a miracle."*

## Part 4 — The Fortress (~25–30k)

- **Approach and recon.** The twins' full workup via relayed Perfect Telepathy — one twin watches, the other relays with full senses. Guard rotations, where Amanda sleeps, where the goblins won't go. Their intel is riddled with absurd Luck-20 accidents the party can barely believe.
- **The plan hinges on the known alarm.** Amanda unknowingly raises alarms (canon) — so she must reach the Null Zone *first* or nothing else works. Stealth fails by design; the plan should go loud.
- **The trade (one disaster, confirmed):** the infiltration's first objective *works* — Amanda reaches the Null Zone, the lattice breaks, she's herself — and the escape collapses. **Wade buys the exit and is taken.** The man who froze at the mirror doesn't freeze this time; this is the mirror-room failure's payoff, and the moment someone later puts an "I WAS BRAVE" sticker on *him*. Symmetry with the book's opening act: Eron captured and tortured Wade pre-Transition; Book 1 ends with Eron taking him again — except everything Eron believes about his prize is wrong.
- **The inversion:** Eron thinks he *gained a hostage and kept his compass*. He actually lost his compass and was handed an agent. Nobody has to talk Wade into the plan he'd never allow — he isn't in the room. Amanda walks back through the gates as the thrall Eron never doubted: *"He came for me. I'm going for him."*
- **Twins glue:** family-sense means the twins know exactly where their father is held. Their recon was for mom all book; now both parents are inside, and the kids who wouldn't leave one parent can't leave either.
- **Finale.** Amanda is the alarm that stays silent, then turns at the critical moment (gate, warning unsent, or breaking Eron's concentration mid-fight — decide in drafting). Twins ride the wolves in canon's promised charge. L25 Warlord + fortress vs. L20 geared party; Eron's Mind Mage kit vs. Clint's aura is the tactical spine. Amanda gains Clint's aura protection once they're reunited (canon).
- **The family reassembles *inside* the walls** — all four of them at the fortress, not after it. When the lattice broke, the twins got a parent back for the second time in their lives — the hospital healing beat, rhymed.
- **Falling action.** Wade steps away from adventuring (canon) — lands because Part 3 showed what losing him costs. Book 2 hooks: Strider's oddities, whatever Dan-thread survives.

## Spoiler discipline

Everything here stays inside Book 1's knowledge line (party knows they're observing a sim with console access; nothing about scans, broadcast, or counterparts). Watch Strider dialogue in prep and finale chapters — no real-world facts leak before Book 2's flash schedule.

## Open drafting decisions

- **The scar.** Amanda's first clear memory when the lattice breaks is likely the stab. What Wade does with that wound — heal it flat or quietly keep the scar — is a character decision made on the page.
- **Do the twins witness the stab?** Present would be devastating; absent may be kinder to the pacing. Undecided.
- **The exact turn moment** for Amanda in the finale (gate / unsent warning / broken concentration).
- **Family-sense wrinkle:** Amanda's sense presumably points at the twins too. Luck 20 explaining why every capture party Eron sends comes back empty-handed and humiliated is both the answer and a running dark-comic beat — canonize if used.
- **How the escape collapses** at the trade (her absence noticed, luck running the wrong way, or another mechanism that fits the fortress design once it exists).
