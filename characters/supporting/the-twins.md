# The Twins

The identical twin daughters of [Wade](../party/wade.md) and [Amanda](amanda.md). They were healed by [Clint](../party/clint.md) before the Transition, which granted them extraordinary attributes. That healing did not just save them — it pulled them back from a terminal childhood disease that had already begun erasing who they were.

## Medical Backstory — Sanfilippo Syndrome (MPS III-A)

Before they were healed, the twins were dying of **Sanfilippo syndrome, Type A** (Mucopolysaccharidosis III-A) — a recessive neurodegenerative disorder often called "childhood Alzheimer's." Untreatable in the real world; fatal in the mid-teens. The twins were on track to die at **~14**.

As identical twins they declined in near-synchrony, the timing eerily mirrored — their Perfect Telepathy meaning they could feel each other slipping.

### Progression (birth → healing)

- **Birth–1 yr — *looks fine*:** Born healthy, milestones on time. Only ignorable signs in hindsight: coarse hair, faint unibrow (synophrys), recurrent ear infections.
- **1–3 yr — *the unnamed delay*:** Speech stalls and stops growing; mild clumsiness. Frequently misdiagnosed (late talker / global delay) until age 3–4. **Diagnosis lands too late to change anything** — both twins already carry it.
- **3–7 yr — *the storm, and the forgetting begins*:** Hyperactivity, fearlessness, aggression, brutal sleepless nights. Regression starts — they lose words they had. **Parental recognition frays here first** — flickers at first, then gone. Wade becomes a stranger to his own daughters *before* the twins lose each other.
- **7–10 yr — *the long quiet*:** The storm burns out into stillness. Speech gone. They no longer know their parents. Falls, stiffening, first seizures.
- **10–14 yr — *the end without the System*:** Immobile, can't swallow safely, recurrent aspiration, profound dementia. Death ~14–15. **This is the brink the system reached them at.**

### Why only the System could do it

Sanfilippo is neurodegeneration, not amnesia — the neurons storing those memories die. No real-world medicine could give the twins their parents back. But the system heals by **reverting them to their scanned template**, restoring body *and* the people the disease erased. When Clint healed them, they **remembered their parents for the first time in years** — and the twin-link snapped back on at once. (See [Wade](../party/wade.md) for how this drives him.)

## Stats

- Health: 99

## Post-Transition

When [Eron Vosk](../villains/eron-vosk.md) captured Wade's family, the twins' supernatural luck led to an unexpected outcome: they were taken in by wolves. Due to their Luck 20, what should have been a dangerous situation became an opportunity.

The twins are developing **druid/hunter** abilities through their bond with the wolves. They are too young to have official classes, but the system allows skills and abilities to develop in the background. Clint can see this progression with his System Identify.

They eventually ride the wolves into battle during the rescue of their mother.

## Abilities

### Perfect Telepathy

Innate Skill. Allows perfect communication, including all senses, between the two twins. At higher levels, it may allow the sharing of skills and abilities.

The twins always know each other's location and feelings.
