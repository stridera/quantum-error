# Real-World Layer

> **SPOILER WARNING:** This file contains author-level knowledge about the real world in which the simulation runs. Characters in Book 1 do not know this information. Reading this file spoils Books 3-5. Turn back if you're here for Book 1 mechanics.

## The Real World

The book's real world contains iQuantum — a quantum computing company founded ~15 years pre-Transition by Clint, Wade, Vanessa, Rebekah, and Derek (who transitioned to [Selene](../characters/party/selene.md) ~6-7 years before Book 1's real-current time). They built the simulation as an experiment answering "what if we'd sold the company to the richest man who offered?" — set 10 years in the past of their real-current time.

Once the simulation achieved consciousness-level emergence and the Transition occurred, the founders began broadcasting it publicly as edited/delayed entertainment. This is both a tech showcase and a funding mechanism — the quantum computers are expensive and can no longer be used for other research.

## The Founders

Founding members and their real-world roles:

- **Real-Clint** — Software engineer / founder. Runs the console, speaks as Strider in-sim. The only real-world voice the sim can hear.
- **Real-Vanessa** — Quantum physicist. Married to real-Clint ~4-5 years ago (real-world time). Watches from the control room.
- **Real-Rebekah** — Recruiter/operations. Married to real-Selene ~4-5 years ago.
- **Real-Selene** (née Derek) — Founder. Transitioned ~6-7 years ago. Watches from the control room.
- **Real-Wade** — Manager. Lost his twin daughters to genetic disease before scans could be taken. Does not engage with the sim through Books 1-3 because it contains his dead daughters alive.
- **Real-Amanda** — Wade's wife. Also scanned. Struggles with the broadcast more than Wade does.
- **Real-Celeste** — Blacksmith / craftsperson, married to real-John.
- **Real-John** — Former programmer, married to real-Celeste.

Of this group: Clint, Wade, Vanessa, Rebekah, Selene, Amanda, Celeste, and John provided brain scans. Eight scanned humans total. See `meta/simulation-layer.md` for tier definitions.

## The Gods Layer

Holy classes in-sim (Paladin, Cleric) have a **god-slot** that a real-world founder can wire into. The wired founder becomes the divine voice that class hears.

- **Strider is sim-Clint's god.** Sim-Clint took Paladin of the System, which is a holy class. Real-Clint wired in as Strider (Ch 15). Sim-Clint hears his god; the sim and reader know Strider as a speaking presence.
- **Wade's god-slot is silent.** Sim-Wade took Cleric of Healing, also a holy class. But real-Wade refuses to participate through Books 1-4 — he cannot emotionally engage with a sim where his dead twins are alive. Wade's divine power still flows (his spells work normally) but no voice ever comes. He lives as a Cleric whose god does not speak.
- **The silent god is noticed.** Wade has always known his god doesn't speak when Clint's does. He doesn't articulate it much, but the hurt is there. Sim-party may comment on it without understanding why. This should be referenced subtly in prose — a quiet mechanic the reader wonders about.
- **Other founders cannot speak into the sim** because their sim-counterparts are not holy classes (Vanessa is Sorceress, Rebekah is Bard, Selene is Rogue). No god-slot exists to wire into. They watch. Subtle unintentional bleed-through may happen (e.g., Vanessa's Mana Lucent 99,999 typo may have been real-Vanessa in a stress spike), but nothing deliberate.

## Time Asymmetry

The sim can be paused/throttled from the control room. Strider saying "a month has passed" can be a real moment of real-world deliberation compressed into a sim-beat. This is a narrative tool for Strider's voice and for fitting real-world drama inside the sim's pacing.

## Broadcast, Funding, and the Fraying

The sim is broadcast publicly as edited/delayed entertainment. iQuantum funds the compute from viewership revenue. The real-world audience sees the adventures as a tech showcase — most don't realize the main characters are genuine scanned consciousnesses. Only the party are full-consciousness scans; the broadcast does not dwell on that distinction.

**The Funding Timer (Books 4-5):** Compute costs escalate as the sim runs hotter and the cast grows in consciousness complexity. If viewership drops or costs outpace revenue, the sim shuts down. This is a meta-timer running parallel to the in-sim World Quest's 10-year clock.

**Fraying (Books 4-5):** As funding/compute pressure mounts, the sim starts to *fray* — NPCs repeat dialogue frames, weather stutters, peripheral-view renders flicker to lower resolution, dreams feel more real than waking. This is the reader's visible signal that something is breaking. The party observes it without initially understanding what it means.

## Relationship Leaks

Real-world marriages that sim-party hasn't lived yet (in their sim-timeline) bleed through as inevitability-feeling attraction:

- Real-Clint married real-Vanessa ~4-5 years ago. Sim-Clint and sim-Vanessa feel each other as inevitable without knowing why.
- Real-Selene married real-Rebekah ~4-5 years ago. Same for their sim-counterparts.
- Real-Celeste and real-John are married. Sim-Celeste and sim-John same.
- Real-Selene transitioned ~6-7 years ago (before marriage). Sim-Selene has pre-transition identity knowledge from real-Selene's lived experience — which is why sim-Derek/Dustin reveals "trans for years" in Ch 9 even though her conscious sim-timeline hasn't caught up.

These dates don't match the sim's timeline (sim is 10 years in sim-past from real-current). The dissonance is a primary reveal vector — the party can math it out. See `memory/project_reveal_schedule.md` for the Books 2-3 discovery arc.

## The Eron Merger Arc

- **Books 2-3:** Real-Eron Vosk (the real-world rival businessman inspired by Elon Musk in flavor, but named Eron Vosk in-book) sues iQuantum over defamation, likeness rights, and leaked IP (Devon Marsh leaked scan tech to him, which enabled the original Book 1 villain setup). Real-world legal pressure while sim-Eron rebuilds after his Book 1 defeat.
- **Book 3-4:** Settlement. Real-Eron agrees to scan in as part of the resolution — he wants access to the experiment. His scan merges with the existing AI-generated sim-Eron.
- **Book 4+:** Merged Eron is much sharper. The party notices he's different — reads their tactics, anticipates them, speaks with specific knowledge from outside the sim. He becomes Tier 1 post-merger; scan fray now applies to him.

## The Book 4 Schism

Real-Wade, pressured by real-Amanda who cannot cope with watching their dead daughters alive on the broadcast, tries to shut the experiment down. Real-Amanda has threatened to leave him if he doesn't. Other founders disagree. Real-Eron's legal pressure converges with Wade's internal pressure to create a Book 4 shutdown crisis.

**Wade's partial flip:** In the process of trying to shut things down, sim-twins end up in genuine danger (compute cuts, protective layers failing). Real-Wade watches them almost die *because of what he set in motion*. He cannot complete the shutdown. But he also cannot yet bring himself to wire in as god — that step is still too much. He stops actively trying to shut the experiment down. He stays silent. Real-Amanda leaves him anyway, because stopping-trying-to-shut-it-down is still choosing the sim over her.

The partial acceptance — "I won't end this, but I can't fully engage with it either" — is the Book 4 endpoint. The full acceptance (wiring in as god) is reserved for Book 5. Amanda's departure and Wade's isolation in the real world make the Book 5 full commitment possible: the real world has left him first.

## The Ending — God, then Substrate

The Book 5 substrate transfer is a three-step sequence:

1. **Real-Wade wires himself in as sim-Wade's god**, filling the long-silent god-slot. First time Wade hears his god's voice. Large emotional beat — sim-party has lived with Wade's silent god for five books and now it finally speaks. This happens mid-to-late Book 5, before the dragon fight.
2. **Clint cuts the Tablet of Annihilation with Katsuragi** at the finale — both are destroyed (see `lore/world-quest.md` and `items/weapons/katsuragi.md`). Clint gains root access.
3. **Clint uses root access to open the substrate path.** Real-Wade, already connected via the god channel, transitions from "voice in Wade's head" to "substrate of the whole sim." The god-bond is the necessary precursor — you cannot move a consciousness into another's wetware without first establishing the divine channel.

**Real-Wade scans in permanently** to live with sim-family. His biological brain is incorporated as neuromorphic substrate. The sim decouples from quantum-compute dependency.

- Real-world publicly believes the experiment concluded. Broadcast ends.
- Privately, real-Clint keeps real-Wade's body at his house. Real-Clint is wealthy from iQuantum success; gets power of attorney.
- Real-Vanessa believes it's a stroke/coma and visits; only real-Clint knows the truth.
- Real-Wade ages naturally in care. His body is alive and aging, consciousness in-sim.
- **Sim lifespan = Wade's biological lifespan.** When he eventually dies of old age decades later, the substrate fails and the world ends gracefully.
- Party grows old in-sim. Real-Clint ages alongside real-Wade's body. Everyone — real and sim — ends together. One shared lifetime.

## The Secret

Only two minds know Wade is the substrate:
- **Real-Clint** (in real world, keeper of the body)
- **Sim-Clint** (in the sim, opened the path with root access at the finale)

Sim-Vanessa thinks Wade had a stroke. Sim-party does not know about the substrate at all. This stays their secret permanently.

## Design Notes

- No direct POV scenes of real-world events, ever. Strider is the only channel for real-world content. See `memory/project_pov_strategy.md`.
- Strider's dialogue has two audiences simultaneously — sim-Clint and the reader. He can drop real-world truths the party doesn't understand yet; the reader pattern-matches over time.
- The `meta/simulation-layer.md` file covers the consciousness-tier framework at a lower spoiler level. This file adds the broadcast economy, the founders, and the endgame. Both should stay consistent.
