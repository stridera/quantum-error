# Metamagic

The eighth [school](../overview.md#schools-of-magic). Magic that modifies other magic.

A Metamage does not cast a new spell — they **edit the one they already know, before it resolves.** Shape, target set, duration, cost, timing. The spell list doesn't grow; the versions of each spell do.

This is why the school is rare rather than popular. It gives you nothing on its own. It only pays out to someone who already has spells worth altering *and* enough control to alter them mid-cast, which means it sits behind two other masteries before it does anything at all.

## Common Edits

| Edit | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Exclusion** | Remove a target from an area effect. The obvious one, and the one everybody wants first. |
| **Hold** | Complete a casting and keep it uncast, resolved but unreleased. |
| **Split** | Divide one casting between two targets at reduced magnitude. |
| **Substitution** | Pay a spell's cost in a different currency (health, stamina, a held charge). |
| **Extension** | Trade magnitude for duration, or the reverse. |

## The Clint Problem (resolves the Ch 5 open flag)

> Previously flagged unresolved in `classes/paladin-of-the-system.md`: *"Is it a school, a console-only grant, or lost at the Transition?"*

**It is a school, and he still has it.** It survived the Transition the way his stat pool did — the *number* is real and the System honors it. What he never had is the [proficiency](../../system/proficiencies.md): the accrued, use-based technique that turns a rank into a capability. It is the [hacked-stats / Novice-hands gap](../../system/proficiencies.md) in its purest form, and the only ability where the gap is total rather than partial.

**Staging:** the beat lands when **Vanessa** opens the proficiency at Novice and is thrilled about it, and Clint quietly runs an Identify on his own sheet and realizes he has been carrying Legendary-tier rank in the school she just fought for. *"Wait — I have that."* He cannot use it. He does not tell her immediately.

## System Identify — Metamagic (Clint's sheet)

The [Help/Inspect](../../system/ui-popups.md) readout Clint pulls on himself. The last panel is the one he sits with.

```text
Metamagic (School)
Magic that modifies other magic. A Metamage does not cast a new spell. They edit the one they already know, in the moment before it becomes true.

Rank: Legendary — Mastered
Proficiency: —
Cost: Varies by modified effect
Targeting: Your own castings, pre-resolution
Effects:
- Alters a casting already in progress: shape, target set, duration, cost, timing.
- Common edits: remove one target from an area effect; hold a finished spell uncast; split a casting between two targets; pay the cost in a different currency.
- Grants no new spells. Grants new versions of the spells you have.

MASTERY RECORD: GRANTED, NOT EARNED
Rank was written directly to your record via administrative console. No proficiency was accrued. You hold the highest rank in this school and none of the technique. The System will honor the rank. Your hands will not.
Recommended action: none available.
```

**Why the last line lands:** every other System readout Clint has ever pulled ends in something he can act on. This one tells him there is nothing to do. He owns a Legendary mastery the way he owns the [grenade in his fanny pack](../../story/inventory-ledger.md) — carried, unexplained, and useless until it isn't.

**Naming note:** Ch 5 prose spells it **"Meta Magic."** Canonical form is **Metamagic** (one word), matching the [school list](../overview.md#schools-of-magic). Aligning the prose is part of the standing Ch 5 revision TODO.
