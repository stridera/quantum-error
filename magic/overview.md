# Magic

Magic fundamentally involves channeling mana to alter one's surroundings. In magical worlds, mana is everywhere, like air.

## Schools of Magic

There are eight major magic categories:

1. **[System](schools/system-magic.md)** — Manipulation of the underlying system/simulation
2. **Healing** — Restoration and divine power
3. **Temporal** — Time manipulation
4. **Elemental** — Fire, water, earth, air
5. **Mind** — Charm, control, telepathy
6. **Force** — Kinetic and physical manipulation
7. **Gravity** — Weight and attraction manipulation
8. **Metamagic** — Magic that modifies other magic

## Universal Access (Magic & Skills)

Everyone has mana, and every basic ability is learnable by anyone willing to put in the work. A fisherman can learn to create light, start a small fire, or clean with magic. The same holds for skills: anyone can pick up a sword, practice stealth, or train field medicine.

Classes don't gate abilities — they **assist**:

- A class grants its spells and skills outright and accelerates progression in its domain. Learning outside your class is possible but much harder — like studying a language with no teacher.
- **Only a class lets you break normal limits.** Unclassed (or off-class) ability caps out at competent-mortal level: a warrior who drills healing magic for years will never heal like a Cleric, no matter how hard they try.
- Extraordinary off-class effort doesn't break the cap — but it may be recognized: pushing hard enough against your class's edges can qualify you to **evolve your class**. *(Canon hook — evolution mechanics deliberately undefined for now.)*

See also [Classes & Professions](../system/classes-and-professions.md) — classless people can still learn and use basic magic and skills; what they lack is class abilities, class leveling, and the limit-breaking that comes with them.
