# Cleanse

| | |
|---|---|
| **School** | Generic Magic |
| **Mana Cost** | 3 |
| **Effect** | Remove dirt, sweat, blood, and odor from a person, their clothing, or a small object |

One of the universally learnable beginner spells — *"a fisherman can learn to create light, start a small fire, or clean with magic"* ([Magic overview](../overview.md#universal-access-magic--skills)). Cheap, unglamorous, and the single most-cast spell in the post-Transition world by an enormous margin.

Cleanse works on the surface state of a thing: grime lifts, blood stops being blood, the smell goes. It does not repair, sterilize a wound, or purify water for drinking — [Create Water](create-water.md) covers that, and healing covers the rest. It does not remove [statuses](../../system/effects.md); the name is a coincidence of vocabulary that annoys clerics.

## Why It Matters

Everyone learns this within a week of the Transition. It is the first spell most people bother with, ahead of light and fire, because the alternative is being filthy forever. Its ubiquity is why the post-Transition world doesn't read as squalid despite the collapse of plumbing and laundry infrastructure.

The party learns it early and off-page. **It is deliberately never a plot point** — it exists so that hygiene never has to be one.

## Ambient Versions

Being cast at is not the same as not having to think about it. Two [holy auras](../../classes/cleric-of-healing.md#cleric-aura) run a continuous, passive equivalent inside their radius, which is strictly better because nobody has to remember:

- **[Wade's](../../characters/party/wade.md) [Cleric Aura](../../classes/cleric-of-healing.md#cleric-aura)** — restoration logic. Walk near him long enough and you come out presentable.
- **[Clint's](../../characters/party/clint.md) [System Aura](../../classes/paladin-of-the-system.md#system-aura)** — correction logic, and it reads completely differently. See both class pages.

See also **[Rejuvenation](../../classes/temporal-bard.md#rejuvenation)**, which handles the *biological* half of the same problem.
