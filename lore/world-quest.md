# World Quest

The World Quest is the series' central timer. An artifact ([Tablet of Annihilation](tablet-of-annihilation.md)) will destroy existence in 10 years unless the world boss guarding it (the Ancient Red Dragon, see `bestiary/ancient-red-dragon.md`) is defeated. The quest appeared at the Transition (Ch 13) and drives the series pace across Books 1-5.

## Origin

At the moment of the Transition, when [Clint](../characters/party/clint.md) changed the world template from Technology to RPG, the quantum computer displayed **"Simulation corruption detected. Reset?"**. Clint watched the prompt appear at the quantum computer terminal.

*(Meta: real-Clint, outside the sim, denied the reset — see `meta/real-world.md`. That denial plus the fear that prompted it is the real-world origin of this quest. The system renders user fears as quests; a cancelled reset becomes a prevented apocalypse.)*

The quantum computer then transformed into the **Tablet of Annihilation** — the physical in-sim manifestation of the reset-threat. An Ancient Red Dragon appeared and destroyed iQuantum HQ, taking the Tablet to its lair (the Saratoga ruins of the building). The World Quest was active from that moment forward.

## Quest Terms

Visible to all players via the system:

- **Objective:** Defeat the Ancient Red Dragon. Destroy the Tablet of Annihilation.
- **Timer:** 10 years from the Transition.
- **Failure state:** Existence ends.
- **Reward:** World persists. Specific rewards per party TBD in narrative drafting.

The quest cannot be abandoned. All scanned and player-tier inhabitants are considered participants by default.

## Series Structure

Each book covers approximately 2 years of in-sim time. The 10-year clock drives the pacing.

| Book | Years (sim) | Levels | Quest Status |
|------|-------------|--------|--------------|
| I — *Quantum Error* | 1-2 | 1-25 | Quest activated; party at regional scope |
| II — *Quantum Breach* | 3-4 | 26-45 | Party expanding, early real-world pressure |
| III — *Quantum Collapse* | 5-6 | 46-65 | Mid-game stakes; party realizes meta-layer |
| IV — *Quantum Fracture* | 7-8 | 66-85 | Eron merger, Wade schism, fraying begins |
| V — *Quantum Singularity* | 9-10 | 86-99 | Dragon fight. Quest resolution. |

## Fraying

In Books 4-5, as real-world compute/funding pressure mounts, the simulation starts to *fray* (see `meta/real-world.md`). This is the in-world signal that the infrastructure supporting the quest is degrading. The quest becomes harder to complete as the world itself starts failing.

By the final fight, respawn is compromised and the sim is actively breaking. The party cannot rely on unlimited deaths to rez-rush the boss. See `memory/project_death_costs.md`.

## Resolution

At the finale:
1. Party reaches the dragon's lair at the Saratoga ruins.
2. Dragon dies (killing blow likely via Katsuragi's Cancel-Reset ability — see `items/weapons/katsuragi.md`).
3. Tablet is reachable in the dragon's hoard, where it has been since Ch 13.
4. Clint cuts the Tablet with Katsuragi. **Both are destroyed** — unstoppable force meets immovable object, mutual annihilation.
5. Root access activates for Clint.
6. Quest resolves. Existence persists.

The destruction of both items is thematic closure: Clint started the series exploiting the system with console-crafted items; he ends it by cancelling those exploits. See `memory/project_series_endgame.md` for the full endgame flow.

## Not Referenced In

Prose canon has touched the World Quest in Ch 13 (appearance) and must reference it in later chapters as a pressure on the party. The *origin* (cancelled reset at the meta-layer) is author-only until the Book 5 reveal — do not explain the quest's origin in-narrative before then.
